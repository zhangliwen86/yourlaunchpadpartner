02 · Grants & funding

02

Not sure what's available? Let's find out.

We'll help you explore what may be relevant to your business — and be straight with you about what isn't. That can mean understanding eligibility, making enquiries for clarification, helping prepare an application where appropriate, helping navigate the process, seeing a funded project through, and coordinating with relevant third-party vendors where needed.

We don't guarantee approval or funding, and we don't submit or manage applications as the applicant — we help you work through the process.
