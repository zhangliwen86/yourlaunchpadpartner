Questions

A few things people ask.

What does the SGD 699 include?

Your place in a small-group, one-day online workshop where you build a real website for your business, with guidance throughout, and support in going live and understanding how to maintain it afterwards.

How many people are in each workshop?

Between 3 and 5 participants. The group is kept small so there's room to actually work through each business, and curated where possible around compatible business profiles.

Is it online?

Yes. The workshop runs over Zoom, in one day.

Do I need technical experience?

No. It's built for non-technical business owners. You don't need to know how to code.

Do I need to bring anything?

Whatever you already have about your business helps — logos, photos, existing copy — but it's not required. The questionnaire beforehand is what actually gets things started.

Will I build my own website?

Yes. You work on your own business — not a demo or classroom exercise — starting with a questionnaire about what you need, then building from there.

What tools do you use?

Claude and Pepita, used as practical tools during the build. The point isn't the tools — it's understanding what gets built and why.

Will my website actually go live?

Bringing the site together and getting it live is part of the workshop.

Will I know how to maintain it afterwards?

That's part of the point. The last stage of the workshop covers understanding what was built and how to keep it updated.

Is this suitable for freelancers?

Yes — freelancers and small business operators are a good fit, alongside SME owners.

What happens after I register my interest?

Tell us a little about your business and what you'd like to build, and we'll get back to you about the next available session.
