Who's behind this

Good businesses don't always need more advice. Sometimes they need someone to work through things with them.

Your Launchpad Partner was built around a simple idea: business owners shouldn't have to figure everything out alone. We help make sense of the numbers, work through what support may be relevant, and find practical ways to make the business easier to run.

Why we built this

Running a business means making decisions with incomplete information.

Sometimes the books aren't where they need to be. Sometimes there may be support available, but it's difficult to know whether it actually applies. Sometimes the problem isn't a lack of effort — it's that too much of the work is manual.

We started Your Launchpad Partner because we believe practical help should begin with understanding the problem, not selling a package.

What we believe

Five things we try to hold to.

01

Start with the problem.

Not the package. We'd rather understand what's actually going on first.

02

Keep it practical.

Advice that doesn't turn into a next step isn't worth much.

03

Be honest.

Including when the honest answer is "not yet" or "not us."

04

Add value.

If we can't, we'd rather say so than fill the time.

05

Treat the business like it matters.

Because it's not just a business to the person who built it.
