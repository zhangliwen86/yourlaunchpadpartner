Meet the pastries

For an everyday treat or a small gift: get to know each pastry before you choose. Look at the outside, then look inside.

Butter croissant

Outside
Deep-bronze, crackly-looking layers curving to two tapered ends.
Inside
Open, honeycomb-like pockets framed by thin, crisp layers.
Flavour inspiration
The classic: rich and buttery, with a toasty edge from the darker folds.
Nice for
A slow morning, or a simple treat to share.

Pandan kaya twist

Outside
A long, flaky twist, bronzed on every turn, with green showing between the folds.
Inside
Soft, pale layers with a green ribbon spiralling through.
Flavour inspiration
Inspired by kaya toast: fragrant pandan with a gentle coconut-custard sweetness.
Nice for
An afternoon kopi break.

Cherry cream danish

Outside
A crisp, square pastry frame folded around a soft centre.
Inside
Many thin layers beneath a creamy middle and glossy cherries.
Flavour inspiration
Bright, tangy cherry against a mellow, creamy centre.
Nice for
A bright treat that feels like a small celebration.

Dark chocolate babka knot

Outside
A tight, glossy knot with crunchy-looking chocolate edges.
Inside
Tender, bread-like layers ribboned with dark chocolate swirls.
Flavour inspiration
Deep, bittersweet cocoa wrapped in soft, lightly sweet dough.
Nice for
Pulling apart and sharing at a small gathering.
