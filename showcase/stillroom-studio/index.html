Modern luxurious and Funky directions · AI-generated illustrative concepts of the same room

  • Storage wall

    Modern luxurious: walnut-toned cabinets and a stone-look panel behind the television, with lit display shelves. Funky: yellow and coral fronts, an open red bookcase and a cushioned bench at the end.

  • Lighting

    Modern luxurious: a brass dome pendant and a soft glow along shelves and plinths. Funky: an orange enamel pendant and a red floor lamp as bright accents.

02 · Everyday choices

Beautiful rooms, built around ordinary days.

  1. Storage

    Where the day starts

    A bench by the door with drawers beneath gives shoes a place to land, and somewhere to sit while you put them on.

  2. Light

    Evenings, not just daytime

    A pendant over the table, softer light along shelves and a lamp by the sofa let each part of a shared room be lit for what is happening there.

  3. Placement

    Room to pass

    A clear route from the door to the window and kitchen, with rugs, lighting and furniture quietly marking out where people eat, rest and work.
